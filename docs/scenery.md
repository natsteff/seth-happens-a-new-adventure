# Scenery

The rooftop city uses an illustrated skyline, river and bridge, textured brick facades, stone roof caps and decorative rooftop equipment.

`src/scenery.ts` handles decoration. `src/mission.mjs` defines the playable route. Background scenery adds no collision surfaces. Roof-cap tops align with the collision geometry.

`public/assets/scenery/sunset-skyline-v1.png` is the distant backdrop. `brick-facade-v1.png` repeats behind buildings, with separate caps and shading. Scenery is stylized rather than geographically accurate parallax.
