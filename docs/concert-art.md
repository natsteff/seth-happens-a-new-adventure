# Concert Chaos artwork

`public/assets/adventure-logo.png` is the game’s displayed logo. `public/assets/title-logo.png` is the earlier logo asset.

`public/assets/concert/stage-v1.png` depicts a fictional singer, guitarist and drummer under concert lighting. There is no real-band likeness or branding.

The stage image is decorative. Photo highlights, timing cues, foreground obstructions and interactive collision geometry are separate game objects. Arena seating, rails, stairs, the credentials desk and pass are drawn in code.

The same logo appears in the page header and opening screen, with responsive sizing and descriptive alternative text.

The active crowd uses four original AI-generated adult concertgoers in `public/assets/cast/concert-v1.png`, plus reused upright fan pairs and a few drunks. Tiny crowd cutouts, sideways airborne figures and clowns were removed from this scene. Metal crowd-control grilles are drawn in code. Broad, fully supported concrete stair flights and a landing wall contain the ascent/descent route; their collision bodies remain separate from railing/crowd art.
