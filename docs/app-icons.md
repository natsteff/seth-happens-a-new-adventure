# App icons

The icon family uses the New Adventure character-and-city artwork in `public/icons/new-adventure-master-v1.png` (1254 × 1254, opaque RGB).

- iPhone: `apple-touch-icon-new-adventure-v1.png`, 180 × 180. The conventional `apple-touch-icon.png` contains the same artwork.
- General app icons: 192 × 192 and 512 × 512.
- Maskable app icons: 192 × 192 and 512 × 512, with additional navy padding to protect the face and lettering under circular masks.
- Browser favicons: 16 × 16, 32 × 32 and 48 × 48.

The Home Screen label is **New Adventure**. The full manifest name is **Seth Happens - A New Adventure**. Icons are opaque and have square corners; the operating system applies its own mask. Existing installations may cache an older icon or name.
